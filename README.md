# 🌹 Juliet — 3D Rose & Love Letter

An interactive 3D romantic keepsake featuring procedural Juliet roses in Three.js, interactive petals, a wax-sealed envelope, and an unfolding calligraphy love letter.

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Demo-Visit_Site-d4af37?style=for-the-badge&logo=github)](https://kakko15.github.io/3D-Flower/)

👉 **Live Link:** [https://kakko15.github.io/3D-Flower/](https://kakko15.github.io/3D-Flower/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **Procedural 3D Juliet Roses**: Real-time petal rendering with Three.js, subsurface scattering tones, stem thorns, sepals, and bloom animations.
- **Interactive Wax-Sealed Envelope**: Click to crack and shatter the golden wax seal with particle physics.
- **Unfolding Keepsake Letter**: Realistic 3D parchment tri-fold opening animation with customizable calligraphy ink text.
- **Atmospheric Effects**: Floating rose petals, dust motes, candle flicker lighting, and film grain overlay.
- **Interactive Dock**:
  - 🌸 Change rose color varieties (Juliet Apricot, Crimson Velvet, White Pearl, Midnight Orchid)
  - ✍️ Edit love letter text & recipient
  - 📸 Capture keepsake screenshot (PNG)
  - 🎵 Ambient soundscape & chime effects
  - 🔄 Camera orbit controls (drag to rotate, pinch/wheel to zoom)
